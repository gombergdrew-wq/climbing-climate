Data: Open-Meteo Historical Weather API, derived from the ECMWF ERA5 reanalysis. Trends are ordinary-least-squares fits over the trailing 30 complete years and are meant to illustrate direction and rough magnitude, not serve as a peer-reviewed climate record. "Prime climbing days" and "too hot to send" are simple daily-high heuristics (10–24°C / >32°C), not a substitute for local route/season knowledge.
